# AI Engineering Journal

This document records the actual AI interactions, human validations, engineering decisions, and verification steps performed throughout the development of the **Student CGPA Performance Management System**.

---

## Interaction Log Template

Copy and fill this template for each interaction or task completed with the AI assistant.

### Entry: [Task Title / Interaction Number]
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Task / Problem**: Concise description of the problem being solved or feature requested.
- **Actual Prompt Given to AI**:
  > Put the exact prompt text here.
- **AI Suggestion**: Summary of the code, explanation, or test proposed by the AI.
- **Changes Accepted / Rejected**: Detail what parts of the AI suggestion were accepted, modified, or rejected.
- **Human Validation Performed**: Specific manual checks, code inspections, or runs performed by the developer.
- **Test Results**: Output or status of Pytest / Ruff verification.
- **Engineering Decision & Reason**: Rationale behind accepting, altering, or rejecting the AI's proposal.
